ClearSpeak · Your information

Your account. Your choice.

Last updated September 13, 2026 · Version 1.0 preparation

You can request deletion from inside ClearSpeak. You do not need to contact support or wait for a subscription to expire.

Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription. If you wish to stop renewal, open Apple subscription settings and cancel ClearSpeak there. You can still proceed with account deletion.

Delete from the app

  1. Open ClearSpeak and go to Profile.
  2. Open the account and privacy controls, then select Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, confirm and complete any requested Apple identity check.

The app submits a deletion request, closes active practice and confirms when the request is queued. A background worker removes the account's profile, transcripts, reports, progress and learning memory, and requests removal of its RevenueCat customer record. Service failures are retried; a pending request should not be shown as completed.

Information that may remain

A restricted cryptographic fingerprint records whether an Apple account has used its lifetime free introduction. It contains no name, email, transcript or recording and prevents repeated free introductions after account recreation. Provider-held purchase, security and legally required records and expiring backups may remain under their own retention rules. Read the retention details.

If you cannot sign in

Contact clearspeak.app@gmail.com with the subject “ClearSpeak account deletion.” Explain how you signed in and share an app account reference if you have one. Do not send an Apple password, authentication token, payment details or private recording. We may request a safe way to verify ownership before removing or disclosing account data.

Want to keep the account?

You can clear learning memory, change goals or disable reminders in Profile. Those controls do not replace full account deletion. This guide describes the version 1.0 app under development; public availability will be announced on the home page.
