These terms describe the upcoming ClearSpeak 1.0 service, operated by Wesley Jaynes. The app is in development and is not yet publicly available. Questions: clearspeak.app@gmail.com.

What ClearSpeak provides

ClearSpeak is communication practice for adults who already converse in English. Authored lessons and an AI coach help you practice a specific communication skill. AI output can be incomplete or mistaken; use your judgment and tell us when feedback is unhelpful. ClearSpeak is not speech-disorder diagnosis or treatment, and does not promise fluency or a particular outcome in 28 days.

Your account and conversations

You must be at least 18 and use your own account. Sign-in and consent are required for live practice. Do not share access, attempt to bypass usage limits, interfere with the service or use it unlawfully. You may use fictional scenarios and should avoid sharing confidential information. You retain your own content; you allow the processing needed to provide the practice and feedback you request, as described in the privacy policy.

The free introduction

An eligible account receives one lifetime free three-minute introduction after onboarding. No purchase is required, and the introduction does not start an auto-renewing subscription. Feedback appears before the membership offer. Additional live AI conversations require verified membership.

Weekly membership

ClearSpeak Plus renews weekly through Apple. Membership includes one main three-minute conversation per local learning day, plus one optional extra three-minute conversation after the main session is finalized. These slots expire at the day boundary and do not roll over. The active conversation window includes coach speech, your speech and ordinary thinking pauses. Technical recovery is subject to the remaining session allowance and server reconciliation.

The App Store's localized price, billing period and purchase confirmation govern the amount charged. The app displays those details before purchase. No price on this information site overrides Apple's confirmation. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple. Review the renewal date and cancellation options in Apple subscription settings. The free introduction is not a subscription trial.

Restores, cancellation and deletion

Use Restore purchases in the app to reconcile an existing purchase. Cancelling a subscription normally leaves access until the verified paid period ends, subject to Apple refunds or revocation. Apple handles refund requests. Deleting ClearSpeak or deleting your account does not cancel the subscription; you may delete your account without waiting for a subscription to expire.

Availability and changes

Live conversations require connectivity and working providers. We may pause new sessions to address an outage, a security problem or service limits. We will aim to explain unavailable or pending states and preserve completed feedback. Features and these terms may change; material updates will be described in the app or on this site.
