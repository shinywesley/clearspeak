ClearSpeak · Your information

A little help, right here.

Last updated September 13, 2026 · Version 1.0 preparation

For questions or a problem you cannot resolve, email clearspeak.app@gmail.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, what happened and roughly when. Please leave out passwords, payment details and private conversation content.

ClearSpeak is in development. The guidance below describes the version 1.0 app. Live practice and purchases are available only in configured test builds until a public release is confirmed.

Microphone and audio

In iPhone Settings, find ClearSpeak and allow Microphone access. Check your speaker volume and Bluetooth output. If an audio route changes, end or reconnect through the app's recovery screen. Microphone permission and consent to AI processing are separate choices.

Choose a voice and how you take turns

Open your coaching preferences to choose among eight AI voices before a conversation. Natural conversation lets you speak and respond without pressing an answer button. Choose Tap when I’m finished if you prefer to keep an answer open until you tap. You can also adjust coaching style and ask for a slower voice.

Practice your own situation

Choose your own situation from the practice screen and describe what you want to rehearse. Guided, mixed and free practice preferences let you choose how much structure you want. You can update your goal and practice needs in your profile. These live conversations use the same introduction or membership allowance as guided lessons.

A conversation disconnected

Return to the app and use the recovery option for the existing session. The server reconciles that session before starting another. A failed start before usable conversation preserves the introduction; an interrupted session uses its remaining time. If closure cannot be confirmed, the app shows a pending recovery state rather than granting a new full allowance.

Daily membership allowance

Membership includes one main three-minute conversation and one optional extra three-minute conversation per local learning day. The extra becomes available after usable main practice is finalized. Unused daily slots do not roll over. Your account timezone controls the day boundary; a timezone change does not create extra sessions. The one-time free introduction is separate from member allowances.

Purchase pending, or access missing

Complete any Apple approval and check your Apple Account. In the app's membership screen, choose Restore purchases and wait for verification. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. A pending or cancelled payment does not unlock paid practice. If access remains incorrect, contact support with a transaction reference and app account ID, not your full receipt or payment card information.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Apple's subscription settings, choose ClearSpeak and follow Apple's cancellation options. Deleting the app or your ClearSpeak account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Apple manages billing and refund decisions; you can request purchase help from Apple.

Feedback is still being prepared

Your conversation may finish before its feedback is ready. Reopen the saved practice to check its status. If feedback cannot be finished, the app explains what is available and offers a practice takeaway. Missing feedback is not a judgment about how you spoke.

A word was misheard

Open Your words from the saved practice to review the retained transcript. If editing is available, correct the intended words and request another feedback check. A corrected transcript can help assess your message; it does not prove what was audible in the original conversation. Full transcripts are available only during the retention period.

Your second try and your progress

A retry is not automatically an improvement. Feedback should explain a supported change, a skill that stayed strong, or one next move to try. Practice XP, weekly goals and a self-reported real-world check-in have different meanings from an assessed speaking observation.

Accessibility and quiet practice

The app includes captions, reduced-motion support, adjustable coaching preferences and local practice cards. Natural conversation is hands-free. Optional tap-to-finish controls let you decide when an answer is complete. You can ask Pip for a hint or more thinking time during a conversation. For an accessibility problem, tell support which screen and assistive feature you use.

Practice without a live conversation

Local practice cards let you rehearse a useful cue on your own. They do not open a microphone connection, use another AI conversation, or produce an automated speaking assessment.

Terms and age eligibility

Read the Terms and Privacy Policy, then choose Agree and continue to accept the Terms. Consent to external AI processing is a separate step. ClearSpeak is currently for adults aged 18 and over. In regions where an Apple age-range check is required, live practice stays unavailable if that check cannot establish eligibility. Quiet local practice does not open a microphone connection.

Account and information

Read the privacy policy or account deletion guide. Email support if you cannot sign in. We may need to verify account ownership; never send your Apple password.
