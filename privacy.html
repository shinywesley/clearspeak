Privacy Policy for ClearSpeak

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

This Privacy Policy describes how ClearSpeak ("ClearSpeak", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects information in connection with the ClearSpeak mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Core Privacy Principles

ClearSpeak is designed with data minimization in mind. We do not require user accounts. We do not build user profiles. We do not sell personal data. We do not operate a database of user audio recordings.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the App, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Audio recordings that you voluntarily submit for speech analysis
  • Text transcriptions generated from those recordings
  • Derived analysis data such as scores, feedback, and progress metrics
  • Basic technical information (such as app version, device type, and operating system version) for reliability, security, and debugging purposes
  • Anonymous or aggregated usage data to improve performance and stability

3. Audio Processing (No Storage Policy)

When you submit an audio recording, it is transmitted to a third-party service provider (OpenAI) solely for the purpose of generating a transcription and analysis.

ClearSpeak does not store your audio recordings. Audio is processed transiently and discarded after processing. ClearSpeak does not maintain a permanent database of user audio files.

Transcriptions and analysis results may be returned to your device for display within the App. Any progress history is stored locally on your device and can be removed at any time by deleting the App.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by ClearSpeak is used solely to:

  • Provide speech analysis, scoring, and feedback features
  • Display progress and history within the App
  • Improve app performance, stability, and user experience
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or security concerns
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations where required

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

5. Data Storage and Retention

ClearSpeak does not require accounts and does not maintain centralized user profiles.

Audio recordings are not stored by ClearSpeak. Any limited technical logs that may exist are retained only for a reasonable period for security, debugging, and operational purposes.

Your local progress data remains on your device and is under your control.

6. Third-Party Services

ClearSpeak uses third-party services, including OpenAI, to perform speech processing and analysis. These providers receive only the data necessary to perform the requested functionality.

ClearSpeak is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Payments and Subscriptions

8. Data Security

You acknowledge and agree that you use the App at your own risk and that ClearSpeak cannot guarantee absolute security.
