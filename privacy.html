Information we use

InformationWhy it is processed
Account informationSign in with Apple identifies your account. Our server verifies Apple credentials and keeps an internal account identifier, consent and authentication records. Apple may provide the name or email you choose to share.
Goals and preferencesYour practice goal, situations, coaching preferences, timezone and learning history personalize lessons and calculate your daily allowance.
Microphone audioAfter your permission and AI-processing consent, audio is transmitted to OpenAI for the live conversation. ClearSpeak does not create a stored raw-audio library. Saved audio clips are not included in this launch implementation.
Transcripts and feedbackText from the conversation supports captions, evidence-based cues, reports and progress. Reports may retain short excerpts from your attempts. Avoid sharing private workplace or sensitive personal details; fictional examples work.
PurchasesApple and RevenueCat process purchase identifiers, transaction and subscription status. ClearSpeak uses this information to verify access and reconcile purchases. We do not receive your payment card number.
Technical and product eventsLimited events describe steps such as a session starting, a report becoming ready or a purchase being verified. IDs, timestamps, status, durations and app versions support reliability, usage limits and conversion measurement. Speech content and raw audio are not included in these event properties.
Support messagesIf you email support, the address and information you provide are used to respond. Do not send passwords, Apple credentials, payment card numbers or private recordings.

Services that help ClearSpeak work

Apple provides sign-in, app distribution and purchases. OpenAI processes voice and bounded assessment requests. Railway hosts the application services and PostgreSQL database. RevenueCat supports subscription verification and lifecycle tracking. GitHub Pages hosts this static information site. GitHub also stores encrypted database backups in a separate private repository’s workflow artifacts, with seven-day retention.

ClearSpeak requests that Live session storage be disabled. This does not mean every provider has zero retention: providers may keep operational, security or legally required records under their own terms. See OpenAI's API data controls, Apple's privacy policy, RevenueCat's privacy policy, Railway's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

Service providers may process information in countries other than yours. ClearSpeak does not sell personal information or use your speech for advertising. This website has no advertising trackers, signup forms or added analytics. GitHub may process request and security logs; fonts and images are served from this site.

How long information stays

The application cleanup worker removes ordinary full transcript fragments and sanitized product events after 30 days. Concise reports, their evidence excerpts, profile preferences and learning history remain until account deletion. No optional audio-clip storage is enabled in this version.

Account deletion removes the application account and associated learning data, closes active sessions and requests deletion of the RevenueCat customer record. Provider retries can delay completion; the app reports deletion as pending until processing completes. Encrypted database backups expire after seven days. If an older backup is restored, later account deletions must be reapplied before service resumes. Provider-required records may persist under their own retention schedules, so deletion is not a promise of instant erasure from every system.

A restricted cryptographic fingerprint of the Apple account identifier and whether its introduction was consumed is retained to prevent repeated use of the one-lifetime introduction. It contains no name, email or speech content. It is kept for as long as needed to enforce that offer; deleting and recreating an account does not reset it. Purchase records held by Apple remain subject to Apple's own obligations.

Your controls

  • Choose whether to consent to external AI processing before a live conversation.
  • Mute the microphone, end the session or revoke microphone access in iPhone Settings.
  • Inspect or clear learning memory and edit preferences in the app.
  • Disable optional reminders in the app or iPhone notification settings.
  • Initiate account deletion in the app; see deletion instructions.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, or request a copy of your information. Contact us to request help. We may verify that you own the account before disclosing or changing it. Where applicable, consent supports optional voice processing; requested account and subscription services require information to provide them; security, offer-abuse prevention and required records serve operational or legal needs.

Adults, security and updates

ClearSpeak is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us. Access controls, encrypted connections and server-side secrets protect information; no system can promise absolute security. Material changes will be reflected here and, when appropriate, in the app.
