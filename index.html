Frequently Asked Questions

Do you store my recordings?
No. ClearSpeak processes your recordings to generate feedback and does not store your audio files.

Do I need an account?
No. ClearSpeak does not require creating an account.

How does the subscription work?
ClearSpeak offers a weekly subscription with a free trial. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.
